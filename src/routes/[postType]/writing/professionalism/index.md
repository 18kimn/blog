Everything that is mine is yours, and it has been that way
since the beginning of it all, before I had any choice in
the matter or an opportunity to pretend it could be anything
else. Everything that I want as a material desire for
myself, is yours, every impulse and luxury I attain and
possess only the property of the billions of us that have
made such experiences possible. My strengths are only the
contributions and investments and love poured into the
personality seen as "Nathan" by those around me; my
accomplishments only the sum total of the accumulation of
all of the preceding modes of production and the successive
resolutions of thousands of historical conjunctures and how
individuals have responded to them (unfortunately Kamala
Harris had a point here). That is part of an objective
reality, a relationship that holds true regardless of my
individual perception of them, though that is undoubtedly
relevant; as a result no matter how much chauvinism and my
ego dominate it is always your right to remind me of this
fact and to observe what I have committed to and hold me to
such.

forever, long after I have left the earth and long after any
