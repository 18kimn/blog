## The 1:1 conversation (you cannot organize in something you do not care about)

    - A1: you cannot facilitate organizing by group chat
      messages, only through the curation of individual
      relationships (and group chat messages are effective
      only to the extent that they facilitate individual
      relationships)
    - A2: asks to people only work if you are brazenly and
      openly honest about what you are asking for. _You
      cannot fake an ask; if you don't believe in it, the
      person you are talking to will not either._
    - being brazenly and openly honest and still having a
      compelling ask means that you cannot ask for small or
      useless things ("come to a meeting" is not itself the
      ask, though it may be an actionable part of a larger
      ask)
    - a compelling ask is an empowering ask, something that
      asks people to take something and win something for
      themselves
    - people cannot be empowered if you position yourself as
      a leader over them who are not
    - people cannot be empowered if you position yourself as
      a helpless mess
    - people have to be empowered by you positioning
      yourself first as them, then as someone competent and
      respectable, then as someone ambitious

## Structure (in defense of the cabal)

    - refer to A1
    - creating individual relationships with purely an
      organic emphasis (i.e. relying on existing and
      autonomous desires within a collective) is uneven, can
      miss opportunities to create new energy, and can leave
      people out
    - creating individual relationships with purely a
      systematic emphasis can be superficial and top-down
    - but neither of those deficiencies are guaranteed, and
      "purely organic" or "purely systematic" doesn't exist
    - A productive organic emphasis meets its systematic end
      by diligently and regularly forming spaces for people
      to create their own connections
    - This still, however, requires self-selection based on
      interest rather than contacting a new organizer
    - A productive systematic emphasis meets its organic end
      by honing the ask (see above) to be honest and
      compelling
    - The premise of this emphasis, however, involves a 1:1
      conversation, and eventually needs to grow to the
      level of spontaneous and autonomous action if a
      movement seeks to be militant and permanently alter
      consciousness (avoid being stuck in a position where
      organizers hand down action items)
    - consistently accomplishing either of those
      aforementioned emphases requires regularly convening a
      group of people
    - to meet its goal of consistent and regular creation of
      spaces, the group should have at least a few members
      who are consistent about their investment

      committee, a position with a title, etc)

## Personal growth (the totally unnatural process of becoming myself)

    - D1. you cannot be a workplace organizer if you are
      averse to hanging out with your coworkers, cannot be a
      tenant organizer if you are averse to hanging out with
      your neighbors
    - D2. structure-based organizing means organizing with
      people that are of vastly different personalities and
      backgrounds (united by material conditions),
    - D3. It also means constantly talking to new people
    - D1+D2+D3 = D4: you need to have somewhat of an
      extroverted demeanor when youre in organizing mode, be
      able to talk to people you don't know super well and
      who are vastly different from you or the people you
      are usually friends with
    - Organizing entails patient listening; listen for 80%,
      speak for 20% is often said, and I think that's a good
      rule of thumb
    - Organizing entails having conversations about people's
      material concerns
    - Organizing entails bringing people to a point where
      they feel they have power
    - Despite D4, the above three points are things that
      even the most extroverted person may be no closer
      towards than an introverted person; they are
      reflective of organizing being an "unnatural" endeavor
    - Thus, even if you are an introverted person, you face
      the same kind of challenge as an extroverted person,
      which is to do a profoundly unnatural thing, something
      that your innate personality does not _by itself_
      equip you for, that you need to be thinking actively
      through, constantly and on-the-spot
