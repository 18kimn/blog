  I wish I took at least a few years to work before grad
  school
date: 2025-02-19
tags: [meta]
---

Bullet-pointed blog post:

- I need more money and savings
- I need a kind of work ethic now that I don't have, I'm
  extremely undisciplined and find it hard to commit to
  arbitrary deadlines and especially those imposed on me by
  someone or something else. I wish I had taken some time to
  become used
- I wish I had had time to read, and build up an interest in
  reading regularly, many years before grad school. Very few
  days go by when I did not wish I was more well-read
-

All of that being said, I am here now -- the work ethic,
knowledge, direction, experience I wish I had coming in I
have no choice but to look for and build now. I didn't
include this in my blog post about regrets because I did not
have the knowledge and hindsight I have now for these to
become clear to me -- they are useful only insofar as
thinking about them can open a new future for myself.
